Paper2AI

Started from a stack of health-report photos as a page-by-page transcription tool into Markdown with page numbers and source links, then grew into a scanning workspace with crop, flattening, AI OCR, side-by-side proofing, and PDF/Markdown export.

WhyWhat I builtRole & AITrade-offsOutcome

Why

Why I built it

Dumping dozens of page photos into an AI at once makes missing pages, scrambled order, and OCR errors hard to catch. Pages need ordering upfront and side-by-side proofing before relying on the text.

What I built

What I built

  • Batch selection, drag-and-drop ordering, zoom inspection, and page-by-page transcription preserving page numbers and source filenames.
  • Switchable Codex CLI and OpenAI-compatible vision backends, showing per-page progress and actual token usage.
  • Integrated from a standalone PWA into a unified scanner where users verify OCR output against the original image before export.

Role & AI

How I split the work with AI

Driven by my own paper backlog, I designed the phone-capture-to-desktop-proofing flow and iterated on implementation with coding agents.

Trade-offs

What testing made me change

  • Moved from an early Android prototype to a shared Web/PWA interface to avoid dual maintenance, while keeping batch gallery selection separate from single-shot camera capture.
  • The local PWA handles ordering, inspection, and proofing, while OCR calls switch flexibly between Codex CLI and OpenAI-compatible vision models.

Outcome

Outcome

Evolved from a standalone photo-to-Markdown utility into an integrated single-service scanning and proofing workspace.
